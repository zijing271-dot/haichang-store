Refund & Return Policy

Last updated:

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase from . If you are not, this policy explains how to return an item and receive a refund.

1. Return Window

You may request a return within 14 days of receiving your order. Requests made after this period may not be accepted.

2. Conditions for Return

  • Items must be unused, in their original condition and original packaging.
  • Proof of purchase (order number or confirmation email) is required.
  • For hygiene and safety reasons, certain beauty products (e.g., opened cosmetics, skincare, or personal-care items) cannot be returned once opened or used, unless they are defective.

3. How to Start a Return

Email our customer service team at with your order number and the reason for return. We will respond with return instructions and the return address. Please do not ship items back before receiving authorisation.

4. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you of the approval status. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

5. Shipping Costs

Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (e.g., wrong or defective item). Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer except in cases of defective or incorrect products.

6. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you.

7. Order Cancellation

You may cancel an order before it has been shipped for a full refund. Once an order has shipped, it is subject to the return process above.

8. Contact

For any refund or return enquiry, reach us at . Our team replies within 1 business day.
